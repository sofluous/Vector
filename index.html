Base Pattern Formula Notes

Regular hex vertex network (your supplied primitive):
dx = sqrt(3)r, dy = 3r/2
For each center C, build hex vertices at pi/6 + k*pi/3.
Draw the explicit line set inside each connected hex.
Tile centers on hex lattice with alternating row offset.
Reference-based diagonal lens petals (idealized reconstruction):
Square tile: u by u. Circle radius r = 0.48u.
Circle centers are the four edge midpoints.
Each adjacent circle pair intersects in one diagonal lens.
Tip inset t = u/4 - sqrt(r*r - u*u/8)/sqrt(2).
For the top-left lens, endpoints are (t,t) and (u/2-t,u/2-t).
Join them with two minor circular arcs of radius r.
Construct the other three lenses symmetrically; do not rotate the tile.
Fill in the line color, without guide circles, outlines, or background masks.
Stroke and accent controls do not affect this filled motif.
Radius and tip gaps are reference estimates, not historical constants.
Reference-based four-ring fans (idealized reconstruction):
R = u/2; radii = R * [1, 0.76, 0.52, 0.28]
C(i,j) = (2Ri + (j mod 2)R, jR/2)
Draw upper semicircles, sweeping left to right.
Paint rows back to front: a background-filled outer fan
hides the lower arcs behind it. Do not stroke its baseline.
Rectangular repeat: width 2R, height R (two rows).
Effective stroke = min(requested stroke, 0.45 * 0.24R).
Ring count stays fixed when repetition changes.
Ratios are estimated from the supplied reference, not historical constants.
Idealized connected-vine reconstruction; not an exact tracing.
W = 4u; H = W * 179/278 (estimated reference proportions).
Two periodic cubic stems form the connected backbone.
Eight curls attach at evaluated stem points with matching tangent directions.
Spiral radius rho(t) = R * (1 - 0.92t*t).
Angle theta(t) = theta0 + winding * 2*pi*turns*t.
Convert short spiral intervals to cubic Hermite segments.
Pointed leaves share their base points with the stems.
Match stem positions and derivatives across each repeated boundary.
Effective stroke = min(requested stroke, 0.018W); accent is unused.
Arrangement and proportions remain approximate; historical context is unverified.
